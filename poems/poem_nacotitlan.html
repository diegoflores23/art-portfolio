NACOTITLAN


Nacotitlan is


Nacotitlan is futures


Nacotitlan is a dream


Nacotitlan is cosmovision


like Sun ra’s Saturn,

a rejection of nationalism 

despite implying it 


Nacotitlan is the afrofuturism of this calendar… this geography… 

The one in front of me 

and down below

Like Cyber Aztlan is the afrofuturism to this cumbia 



To write in Spanish

this tongue weighs down 

with heavier contradiction

 


Ya te crees de nacotitlan 


Tu verdad objetiva 


Entre cerros y piedra explotada 


Mi objetividad es futuros  


Concreto como los muros de nacotitlan
